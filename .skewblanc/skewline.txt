Core Structure for all Hierarchies
Category → SubCategory → Domain 

Example
Category: Education
SubCategory: Secondary, College, University, Primary
Domain: Content or Exams or Students or Lectures or etc 

Each Hiarachy contains the Core Structure [ Category → SubCategory → Domain ] 
Exam Hierarchy
Program → Course → Subject → Unit → Chapter → Lesson → Topic → Concept

Example
Program: Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (BSc EE)
Course: Power Systems Engineering
Subject: Electrical Machines
Unit: AC Machines
Chapter: Synchronous Generators
Lesson: Operation and Construction of Synchronous Generators
Topic: Voltage Regulation of Synchronous Generators
Concept: Effect of Load on Terminal Voltage


Institutional Hierarchy
Institution → School → Department → DepartmentSection → Program → Cohort → Class → AcademicYear → Term

Example (University)
Institution: University of Nairobi
School: School of Engineering
Department: Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering
DepartmentSection: Power Systems Unit
Program: BSc Electrical Engineering
Cohort: 2024 Intake
Class: Level 300 – Group A
AcademicYear: 2025/2026 Academic Year
Term: Semester 2


Assessments Hierarchy
Assessment → Exam → Section → Question

Example
Assessment: End-of-Semester Examination
Exam: Electrical Machines II – Semester 2 Exam
Section: Section B – AC Machines
Question: “Explain the impact of synchronous generator excitation on voltage regulation under varying load conditions.”
